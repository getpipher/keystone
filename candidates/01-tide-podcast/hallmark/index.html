About the show

One host, one microphone, mostly outdoors.

Tide is recorded on the coast, at the pace the weather allows. There is no panel, no news segment, no run sheet — just a long walk and a longer conversation with someone whose working life is shaped by water.

Episodes are released every other Thursday and read best in one sitting, the way the tide reads: in, out, and in again.

You can hear the harbour change its mind in October, if you stand in the right place.

— Ep. 019 · The Harbour in October
