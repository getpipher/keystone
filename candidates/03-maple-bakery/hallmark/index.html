The ovens come on at three. By seven the first loaves are out — country, seeded rye, and whatever the season talked us into — and by one, most days, the shelves are quiet again. We have been baking on Maple Street since 2019.

Visit

214 Maple Street, between the bookshop and the old cinema. Tuesday to Sunday, seven until one — or until the shelves go quiet. Closed Mondays. The bench out front is first come, first served, and it is the best seat for a still-warm baguette.

About the baker

Maple Street Bread is a two-oven bakery run by Mara Ellison and whoever she can convince to shape loaves at four in the morning. The flour is stone-milled, the levain is eleven years old, and nothing is baked to order — we bake what we bake, and we bake it well.
