Visit

48 Maple Street, corner of Wren. Tuesday to Saturday, seven until two — earlier close on Saturdays when the last loaf goes. Cash and card. The good bench is out front; there is coffee next door if the line is long.

About

Two bakers, one wood-fired deck oven, and a twelve-hour ferment on everything. We bake what the flour wants that week and sell it on the street it was baked on. That is the whole business plan.
