We design for what comes after.

  1. 01. Durability is the only green that matters.
  2. 02. A product that cannot be repaired is designed to be wasted.
  3. 03. We choose materials for what they become, not how they photograph.
  4. 04. No seasonal drops. No fake scarcity. No landfill as a business model.
  5. 05. We publish where everything goes — the mill, the assembly floor, the grave.
  6. 06. Our roadmap is a repair manual.

Meridian makes a small number of things and keeps making them: a water bottle with a published parts list, a jacket whose seams open cleanly, a lamp that outlives its trend. When one of them breaks, we would rather you fixed it than forgave it.

Nothing we make is finished. It is in use.
