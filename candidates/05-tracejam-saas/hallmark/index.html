Questions engineers actually ask

Does Tracejam sample?

No. Head-based sampling hides the one slow request you needed. Tracejam records locally and lets you decide what leaves the machine.

What can it instrument?

Anything that emits OTLP, plus auto-instrumentation for HTTP frameworks in Node, Go, Python, and Ruby. Raw log lines parse too.

Where does the data live?

On the collector you run — your VPC or your laptop. The hosted workbench reads from it; it never stores your spans.

How is the Team plan counted?

Engineers with an account. Services, traces, and retention are unlimited on every paid plan.
