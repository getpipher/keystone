I’m Anya. I design software systems that people can hold in their heads — distributed back-ends, data platforms, the unglamorous middle where products actually live. The diagram comes before the code, always, and it usually survives both.

What I do

Architecture reviews for systems that have outgrown their original design. Green-field system design for teams that would rather get it right once. And the slower work: sitting with a team until the trade-offs are theirs, not mine — an architecture nobody on the team can explain is an architecture nobody can keep.

How I work

  • i. Measure before you redesign. The dashboard knows more than the deck.
  • ii. Boring technology, deliberately chosen. Excitement belongs in the problem, not the stack.
  • iii. The diagram is the contract. If it has drifted from the code, one of them is lying.
  • iv. Delete more than you add. Every system I admire got smaller over time.

Now

I take one new engagement per month, and 2026 is booked through the autumn. If you have a system that has outgrown its diagram — or is about to — write to hello@anya.dev. I answer everything, slowly and in full sentences.
