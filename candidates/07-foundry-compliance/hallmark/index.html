Questions founders actually ask

Do you replace our auditor?

No. Foundry prepares and maintains the evidence; your auditor audits. We are friendly with several firms and can introduce you.

How long until we are audit-ready?

Teams with their cloud and code connected typically see evidence flowing in the first week. The audit calendar depends on your auditor, not on us.

What happens to our data?

Read-only connections, encrypted at rest, deletable on request. We sell the outcome, not your metadata.

ISO and SOC 2 together?

They share most controls. You map them once; the overlap is evidenced once and reported twice.

We already have policies.

Good — import them. Foundry maps your documents to controls and tells you which evidence each one still needs.
