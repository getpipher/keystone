Asked at every salon

How is Cohort different from an LMS?

An LMS manages content after the fact. Cohort is the live room and the weeks between — built around the conversation, not the gradebook.

How large can a cohort be?

Thirty is where the conversation starts; five hundred is where the room still works. Beyond that we will talk you into two rooms.

Do you take a cut of ticket sales?

No. Tickets are yours — the platform fee is flat and public, on the pricing table above.

Can I bring an existing course?

Yes — sessions, reading lists, and rosters import. Most hosts move over between terms.
